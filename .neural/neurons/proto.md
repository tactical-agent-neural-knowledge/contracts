- `proto/tank/workspace/v1/workspace.proto` `ListMembersRequest.user_ids` (field 5, 2026-10-25): fetch specific members by id, at most 100, whatever the page — a client that meets an id it has never loaded (a crew agent created mid-session, a member past the first 200 in bootstrap) asks for exactly those.
