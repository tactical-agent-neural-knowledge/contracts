# Neurons · proto

refreshed 2026-10-07 · 77435ec229ee

- 25 domains, one package each, always `proto/tank/<domain>/v1/<domain>.proto` with package `tank.<domain>.v1`. Generated Go lands at `gen/go/tank/<domain>/v1/` with the Connect client in a `<domain>v1connect/` subpackage.
- Product vocabulary never reaches the wire. `proto/tank/channel/v1/channel.proto:7`: a Channel "is presented as a 'Tread' in the product… The wire name stays Channel." A board is not its own channel kind; `tank.board.v1` and `tank.canvas.v1` are domains in their own right, attached to a channel by `channel_id`.
- `board.proto`, `canvas.proto` and `books.proto` are the three newest domains (Neuralboards, Neuralcanvas, Neuralbooks) and all three share one shape: a staleness score (0..100) plus a one-sentence `staleness_note`/`moved_note` saying what moved, a `base_rev`/`base_revs`/`base_version` field for collision detection instead of locking, and a `thread_root_id` tying the artifact back to where it was asked for.
- `proto/tank/agentctl/v1/agentctl.proto` is the runner ↔ control-plane contract only: the in-sandbox runner talks to agent-control and never to the messaging core, authenticated by the per-run `RUN_TOKEN`, and all ids are Snowflake decimal strings. It is the biggest file now (close to 900 lines) because every product surface (board, canvas, books) gets its own Runner-side RPCs here rather than the runner calling the domain services directly.
- `RunContext` (agentctl.proto:73) is additive by convention, not just by the breaking-change rule: `skip_neural_knowledge`, `task_class`, `channel_purpose`/`channel_goal`, `repo_access` and `product_run` were all appended as field 20+ rather than inserted, so older runners parsing a newer context never choke.
- `StartRunRequest.mode = "neural"` (agentctl.proto:697) is the Neural Knowledge refresh path: it skips planning and works in `repo` regardless of what the Tread is bound to. `ControlService.RefreshNeuralKnowledge` is idempotent per repository — it keeps one standing thread and refuses a second concurrent refresh rather than racing two.
- `optional` is used throughout as deliberate presence, not nullability: unchanged-field semantics in `UpdateChannelRequest`, "this replaces nothing" in `DecideBenchmarkRequest.supersedes_id` (topo.proto), the empty-vs-unconfigured distinction in `TopoPreferences.configured` (workspace.proto). Dropping `optional` to tidy a field silently changes meaning.
- `proto/tank/workspace/v1/workspace.proto` is still the biggest hand-maintained surface (719+ lines) and `GetBootstrap` is its point: one call on app open, everything else lazy.
- `go_package` is set by managed mode (`buf.gen.yaml`'s `go_package_prefix`) for most files, but seven set `option go_package` explicitly: `billing`, `books`, `canvas`, `catalog`, `monitor`, `platform`, `topo`. A new hand-written one must match the managed prefix exactly or `gen/go` lands in the wrong directory.
- `proto/tank/events/v1/events.proto` is the NATS JetStream envelope: `Envelope.id` is the `Nats-Msg-Id` idempotency key, the payload is a `google.protobuf.Any`, and subjects are `evt.{ws}.ch.{channel}`, `.thread.{root}`, `.user.{uid}`, `.ws`. `AuditLogged` is internal — the gateway never fans it out to clients.
- `proto/tank/blocks/v1/blocks.proto` is the agent UI vocabulary — `PlanCard`, `DiffPreview`, `CiStatus`, `ApprovalPrompt`, `ToolLog`, `StatusCard`, `FilePreview` — plus `GateKind` (PLAN, SCOPE_CHANGE, MERGE, DEPLOY, DESTRUCTIVE_TOOL, BUDGET_INCREASE). A `BlockAction` travels client → server → owning agent as an event, not as an RPC.
- Cross-package imports are real and make FILE-level breaking bite across domains: `agentctl` alone imports `agent`, `blocks`, `board`, `books` and `canvas`; `topo` and `search` import `tank.message.v1`; `admin` imports `tank.workspace.v1` and `tank.channel.v1`. A field removed from a widely-imported file fails the breaking check in every file that merely references it.
- Adding a domain needs only a new `proto/tank/<domain>/v1/<domain>.proto` plus `make gen`: managed mode supplies `go_package` unless you opt into a hand-written one, and lint's `PACKAGE_VERSION_SUFFIX` exemption is already in `buf.yaml`. The TS barrel at `gen/ts/src/index.ts` is hand-written and will not pick up a new domain on its own.
- Never renumber or reuse a field number, and never remove a field before every client has shipped without it (`CLAUDE.md`). `make breaking` / the PR's breaking check catches the first; nothing but discipline catches the second.
